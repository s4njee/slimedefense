# Credits

require. Every entry here must also appear in the in-game credits screen built
in Phase 7.

## Textures

Terrain grass and dirt textures come from Poliigon. Their license terms still
need to be confirmed and recorded here.

## Attribution Requirements

CC BY requires the title, the author, the source, the license, and a statement
of what was changed. Keep all five parts in each entry — dropping the
modification notice or the license link makes the attribution incomplete.
